PRIVACY POLICY

Public notice · Website and business communications

At Archon Tekton, a system should make life easier without making personal information harder to protect. We design and run practical systems for real people. This notice explains what information we may handle through our company website and direct business communications, why we handle it, and how to contact us about it.

1. Who we are

Archon Tekton (Pty) Ltd ('Archon Tekton', 'we', 'us') is a private company incorporated in South Africa under registration number 2026/591074/07. We operate remotely from Gauteng, South Africa. Our business and privacy contact is moagi@archontekton.com; telephone +27 72 493 6690; business WhatsApp +27 72 493 6690.

Business address for statutory privacy notifications and legal correspondence: 152 Capricorn Road, Johannesburg, Gauteng, 2018. This is our registered office for legal and statutory notices; the company operates remotely.

2. What this notice covers

This policy covers visits to archontekton.com, communications you choose to send to our company by email or WhatsApp, and related business correspondence. Our initial public website does not offer visitor accounts, a web enquiry form or customer-facing application integrations. This notice does not claim that a planned messaging platform is already processing customer data.

If we later activate a client platform or Meta/WhatsApp Business Platform API integration, we will update this policy and provide service-specific information about categories, recipients, lawful purposes, retention and deletion before that processing begins.

3. Information we may receive

  • Contact details you send us: name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, organisation, role and preferred contact method.
  • Enquiry and correspondence details: the questions, proposals, documents or other information you voluntarily include in emails or WhatsApp messages.
  • Basic technical information involved in serving the website: for example IP addresses, request timestamps, device/browser signals and security or delivery logs processed by hosting and network providers, where their services generate them.
  • Administrative records of business discussions, service requests and privacy/deletion requests where necessary to answer, manage or evidence them.

Please do not send identity documents, banking details, medical information, children’s information or other sensitive details in an initial enquiry unless we specifically request an appropriate secure channel for a necessary purpose.

4. Why we process information

  • To respond to questions, communicate with potential customers and consider requested services.
  • To arrange business discussions and prepare requested proposals or contracts.
  • To secure, deliver and maintain the public website and protect against misuse.
  • To meet lawful duties, maintain necessary company records and respond to access, correction, objection or deletion requests.

We rely on appropriate grounds under applicable South African law, including your consent where required, steps requested before entering into a contract, performance of a contract, legal duties, or our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and operating a secure business, as applicable. We do not treat this notice itself as consent to unrelated marketing.

5. Email, WhatsApp and third parties

Selecting an email link opens your chosen mail service; sending a message shares the content with the email service providers involved. Selecting the WhatsApp link (https://wa.me/27724936690) opens WhatsApp, whose own terms and privacy information apply to its service. Archon Tekton receives information you decide to send us in those channels.

Website hosting, domain/DNS services, business email providers and messaging platforms may process limited technical or communication data on our behalf or as independent providers. These providers may operate outside South Africa. Before activating new vendors or international transfers, Archon Tekton will assess the arrangements and safeguards applicable under POPIA.

We do not sell the personal information received through the public company website. We will not publish customer correspondence or identify clients publicly without authority.

6. Cookies, analytics and automated decisions

The planned Phase 1 company website does not intentionally deploy marketing pixels, behavioural advertising tools, visitor accounts or analytics cookies. Basic network and hosting technologies may still handle standard technical data. This statement must be rechecked against the deployed site and every third-party embed before launch.

A simple email or WhatsApp contact link is not an automated conversation system. We do not use solely automated decisions to determine outcomes from public-site enquiries at launch.

7. Keeping information and protecting it

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose, legal duty, contract, permitted business record or dispute to which it relates. Routine, unconverted enquiries are proposed to be reviewed for deletion 12 months after the last substantive interaction, subject to justified exceptions. Other records may require longer retention under applicable law or agreement.

Our internal retention standard calls for access controls, strong account authentication, limited sharing, secure disposal, appropriate service-provider review and a record of deletion decisions. No organisation can guarantee absolute security; if we become aware of a security compromise affecting personal information, we will assess and notify as required by law.

8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may ask whether we hold information about you, request access, seek correction or deletion, object to certain processing, and submit a complaint to the Information Regulator (South Africa). A deletion request may not require us to erase records that we must lawfully retain.

Send your request to moagi@archontekton.com with the subject 'Privacy request' or 'Delete my data'. Please provide only enough information to locate the relevant communication. We may take proportionate steps to verify that the information belongs to you before disclosing or deleting it.

The Information Regulator can be contacted via https://inforegulator.org.za/. Data subjects can also consult the prescribed POPIA forms and guidance available from the Regulator.

9. Children’s information

Our public company website is directed at business audiences and does not intentionally invite children to submit personal information. If we discover that we received information relating to a child without an appropriate lawful basis, we will assess it and delete or restrict it where required.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update the public version if our systems, data practices or legal obligations change. This version is effective from 2 November 2026.
