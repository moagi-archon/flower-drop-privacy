DELETE YOUR DATA

Public data deletion instructions · For company contact and applicable Meta-connected services

You can ask Archon Tekton to delete personal information we control. We will treat the request respectfully, check what records we hold, and explain what we can remove or must keep.

1. How to make a request

  1. Email moagi@archontekton.com with the subject 'Delete my data - Archon Tekton'.
  2. Tell us the email address, telephone number or WhatsApp number you used to contact us, and whether your request concerns email, WhatsApp, the company website or a specific connected service.
  3. Do not send a national ID number, bank statement or unnecessary sensitive information. We may request proportionate verification if needed to protect another person’s records.
  4. We will acknowledge the request, check the systems Archon Tekton controls, and respond with the outcome or the reason lawful restrictions apply.

2. What can be deleted

Depending on what we hold, this may include correspondence, contact details, records relating to a closed enquiry and other personal information that is no longer necessary and has no valid retention basis.

3. What might be retained

We may need to keep narrowly limited information where a law, accounting obligation, unresolved dispute, contract or defensible security requirement allows or requires it. We will explain the general reason. We cannot directly erase information independently retained by your email or WhatsApp provider or by other organisations outside our control.

4. Meta, Facebook and WhatsApp connections

At the public company website launch, our WhatsApp button only opens a chat with our business account; it is not a Meta Login or WhatsApp Cloud API integration. If you later use a Meta-connected Archon Tekton application, tell us the relevant product or connected account so that we can identify applicable records.

Removing an app or disconnecting an integration through your Meta account may stop future access, but may not erase records already lawfully held by a separate business. You may also contact us directly to request deletion of information under our control.

5. Processing and response

We aim to acknowledge deletion requests within five business days and provide a substantive outcome within 30 calendar days where practicable. This is an internal service target, not a statement that every request has the same statutory deadline. If more time or verification is needed, we will explain why.
