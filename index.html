Flower Drop Privacy Policy Last updated: 6 July 2026 Flower Drop is a WhatsApp ordering service that helps customers browse products and place orders with our florist business. This policy explains what information we collect through the service, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and in plain language, because that is how privacy policies should be. Information we collect When you interact with Flower Drop on WhatsApp, we receive and store: Your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name associated with it. The messages you send to the service, including product selections and free-text requests. Order details you provide, such as the items ordered, order totals, and payment references. Delivery-related details you choose to share, such as a collection or delivery arrangement. We do not collect payment card details. Payments are made by direct bank transfer (EFT) to our business account, and we only see the payment as it appears on our bank statement. How we use your information We use the information above solely to operate the ordering service: To respond to your messages and show you our product catalogue. To create, confirm, and track your order. To match your EFT payment to your order using the payment reference. To communicate with you about your order's status. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for marketing. Third-party services Flower Drop runs on infrastructure provided by third parties, which process data on our behalf: WhatsApp / Meta Platforms — messages are delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform and are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. Hosting providers — our order-processing software runs on cloud hosting services, which store data securely on our behalf. How long we keep information We keep order records for as long as reasonably needed for business and accounting purposes, after which they are deleted. Conversation messages that are not part of an order are not retained longer than needed to operate the service. Your rights In line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may ask us to: Tell you what personal information we hold about you. Correct information that is inaccurate. Delete your personal information, where we are not required to keep it. To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below. Contact us If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us on WhatsApp at our business number, or by email at moagi@archontekton.com. This policy applies to the Flower Drop WhatsApp ordering service. We may update it from time to time; the "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
